Varietal 01

LITCHI

"Light, floral, morning-bright."

Litchi-blossom honey, harvested in early spring when orchards burst into delicate white blossoms. Pale golden in color with bright citrus notes.

MUSTARD

"Deep, warm, quietly bold."

Mustard-blossom honey, the classic winter harvest. Rich amber viscosity with earthy warmth and a velvety, resonant finish.

Harvest December / Mid-Winter
Profile Warm & Resonant

Harvest Annual Forage Only
